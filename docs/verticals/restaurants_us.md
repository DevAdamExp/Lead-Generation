# US Restaurants — How the Business Works, Compliance & Pain Points

> Reference for the pitch engine. **State/local-variable items flagged `[STATE]`;
> verify current rates/rules before relying.**

## 1. How the money actually works
- **Brutal margins**: net **3–9%**; high volume, perishable inventory, labor-heavy,
  cash-flow fragile. A slow week or a commission leak is felt immediately.
- Revenue mix: dine-in + takeout + **delivery apps** + catering/events.
- Owner/GM is time-poor and on-site. Reach **off-peak 2–5pm**; **phone / Instagram DM** >
  email (email often unread).

## 2. Licensing & permits
- **Business license**, **food service/establishment permit** (local health dept),
  **food handler cards** + a certified **food protection manager** (ServSafe) `[STATE]`.
- **Liquor license** via **state ABC** — expensive, often quota-limited, slow; a major
  asset and compliance burden. **Dram-shop liability** for over-serving.
- **Health inspections** (often **letter/number graded**, sometimes posted publicly).
- Fire/occupancy permit, sign permit, **resale/seller's permit**, grease-trap/wastewater,
  **music licensing** (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC) for played music.

## 3. Taxes (the burden)
- **Sales tax on prepared food** — collected/remitted, rules differ from groceries
  `[STATE]`; a frequent **audit** area.
- **Excise tax on alcohol**.
- **Payroll + tipped-wage complexity**: federal tipped minimum **$2.13 + tip credit**, but
  many states require full minimum or differ `[STATE]`; **tip pooling/distribution rules**;
  **FICA tip credit**; **Form 8027** tip reporting for large food/beverage establishments.
- Income tax + **quarterly estimates**.

## 4. Mandatory recordkeeping & data retention (what they MUST save)
| Record | Why / who | Typical retention |
|---|---|---|
| **POS / daily sales** records | sales-tax filing, audits | 3–7 yr |
| **Tip records**, 8027, allocated tips | IRS | 3–4 yr |
| Payroll, **W-4, I-9**, schedules | IRS/DOL/FLSA | 3 yr (FLSA) / I-9 rules |
| Food cost / inventory / supplier invoices | COGS, audits | 3 yr |
| **Health inspection** reports, temp logs, **HACCP** logs | health dept | varies (often 1–3 yr) |
| **Allergen** info, recipes | FDA Food Code / liability | ongoing |
| Alcohol purchase/sale logs | ABC | per state |
| Card data handling | **PCI-DSS** | ongoing compliance |

→ Most independents run on a **POS + spreadsheets + the owner's head**. Data is siloed in
the POS and delivery apps — an integration/reporting pitch.

## 5. Labor & food-safety compliance
- **FLSA** overtime, **tipped-wage** rules, **minor labor** limits, **ACA** (50+ FTE),
  some cities have **predictive-scheduling** laws `[STATE/CITY]`, workers' comp.
- **FDA Food Code** (adopted by states), allergen labeling, manager certification,
  local inspections/grades; **HACCP** for certain processes.

## 6. Operational process flow (end to end)
`Concept → licensing/buildout → menu engineering & food costing → supplier setup →
staffing (FOH/BOH) → daily service → POS/inventory/labor scheduling → delivery + reservations
→ reviews/reputation → loyalty/repeat`.
Leak points: **labor scheduling, food cost, no-shows, commission bleed, reputation swings,
no repeat-customer engine**.

## 7. Pain points → where an agency sells
| Pain | Sell |
|---|---|
| **Delivery-app commissions 15–30%** | **direct online ordering** (lead with the $ figure) |
| Empty seats slow nights | reservations/waitlist, off-peak promos, email/SMS |
| Reputation swings (bad-review clusters) | review management + response |
| No repeat-customer engine | **email/SMS loyalty**, first-party data capture |
| Weak/old site, hard-to-find menu | site + Google menu/profile optimization |
| Dead/low Instagram (food is visual) | social content + photography |
| Labor cost/turnover | scheduling tools (partner/upsell) |
