# US Construction — How the Business Works, Compliance & Pain Points

> Reference for the pitch engine. **State-variable items are flagged `[STATE]`; verify
> current thresholds/rates before relying.** No federal contractor license exists —
> licensing is state/local.

## 1. How the money actually works
- **Project-based, high ticket** ($5k handyman job → $1M+ commercial). One extra closed
  job pays for marketing many times over → contractors are premium lead-gen buyers.
- **Margins**: gross 20–35%, net often 5–15% after overhead; thin enough that a few bad
  bids or slow-pay jobs hurt badly.
- **Cash flow is the #1 killer**: they front labor + materials, then wait 30–90 days to
  get paid, with **retainage** (5–10% held until project end). "Profitable but broke" is
  the norm.
- Owner is usually an ex-tradesman, on job sites all day. Office run by spouse/office
  manager. Reach 7–8am or after 5pm; **text beats email**.

## 2. Licensing & permits
- **Contractor license**: state-issued (e.g., CSLB in CA, RMO/RME concepts), usually
  required above a $ threshold `[STATE]`. Some states have **no state GC license** but
  cities/counties do. Specialty trades (**electrical, plumbing, HVAC**) are almost always
  licensed and exam-gated. License lookup is **public** → returns business name, owner/
  qualifier, license type, status, **issue date (= years licensed)**, bond status.
- **Bonds**: license/surety bond required to hold a license; **performance & payment
  bonds** required on bigger/public jobs.
- **Building permits**: pulled per-job from the **local** building dept/county; triggers
  **inspections** (rough-in, final) and a **Certificate of Occupancy**. Permit portals are
  often public → real signal of job flow.
- **Worker eligibility**: I-9 for all; **E-Verify mandatory in some states** `[STATE]`.

## 3. Taxes (the burden)
- **Entity**: sole prop / LLC / S-corp / C-corp. Pass-throughs pay **self-employment tax
  15.3%** + income tax; **quarterly estimated taxes**.
- **Payroll taxes**: employer FICA match 7.65%, **FUTA**, **SUTA** `[STATE]`.
- **Sales/use tax on materials is genuinely confusing** and a real pain: whether the
  contractor pays tax on materials or charges the customer depends on **lump-sum vs
  time-and-materials** contracts and **real-property vs tangible-personal-property** rules,
  all `[STATE]`. Frequent audit area.
- **Subcontractors**: collect **W-9**, issue **1099-NEC** for $600+. **Worker
  misclassification** (treating employees as 1099) is a top IRS/DOL penalty risk.
- **Prevailing wage / Davis-Bacon** on federal & many public projects → **certified
  payroll** reporting (weekly WH-347).
- **Long-term contract accounting**: IRS rules push larger contractors to
  **percentage-of-completion** vs completed-contract `[STATE/threshold]`.

## 4. Mandatory recordkeeping & data retention (what they MUST save)
| Record | Why / who requires | Typical retention |
|---|---|---|
| Signed contracts, **change orders**, bids | dispute/legal, revenue recognition | life of project + 3–7 yr |
| **Lien waivers & preliminary notices** | mechanics' lien rights / cash flow | per state lien deadlines |
| **Certified payroll** (public jobs) | Davis-Bacon/state | 3 yr+ |
| Payroll records, timecards | FLSA | 3 yr (FLSA) |
| **I-9** forms | USCIS | 3 yr after hire / 1 yr after term |
| **OSHA 300 / 300A / 301** injury logs | OSHA | **5 yr** |
| Safety/training records, toolbox talks | OSHA | varies |
| **Certificates of Insurance (COIs)** from subs | liability transfer | life of project + |
| W-9 / 1099 for subs | IRS | 4 yr |
| Permits, inspection sign-offs, CO | building dept | life of structure |
| Warranties, as-builts | warranty claims | warranty period (often 10 yr) |
| Job-costing / equipment depreciation | tax/accounting | 3–7 yr |

→ **This is a goldmine of pain**: most contractors track this in **paper folders, trucks,
texts, and a shoebox**. "Job folder chaos" = a real automation/CRM pitch.

## 5. Labor & safety
- **OSHA**: construction is high-hazard; **fall protection is the #1 cited standard** every
  year. Scaffold, ladder, trenching, electrical, PPE, hazard communication.
- **Workers' comp**: mandatory in nearly every state `[STATE]`; rates by trade class code.
- Apprenticeship/licensing for trades; minor-labor limits.

## 6. Insurance & liability
General liability, **workers' comp**, commercial auto, **builder's risk**, umbrella;
**bonding**. Subs must carry their own + name the GC as additional insured (COI tracking).

## 7. Operational process flow (end to end)
`Lead → site visit/estimate → bid → contract & deposit → permits → material procurement →
schedule subs → build (with inspections) → change orders → punch list → final inspection/CO
→ invoice → collect + lien waivers → retainage release → warranty/callbacks`.
Every arrow is a place work leaks: **slow estimates, lost leads, change-order disputes,
scheduling gaps, slow collections**.
